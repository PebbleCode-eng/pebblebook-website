PebbleBook is a baby and childhood memory app designed for busy parents. It is The Frictionless Memory Keeper. The anti-scrapbook. It’s for the mom who loves the idea of baby books but hates the guilt of an empty one. Gather memories, pebble by pebble, and you are slowly writing the story of them.

Helping you commemorate your children's childhood

#1

Gentle nudges

We give you nudges so you remember to write down the things we think we will always remember. Just in case we don't.

#2

Built for siblings

The app works beautifully with one child, but we are also built especially for multiple children. You can even enter memories for them all at the same time that is saved individually.

#3

Their story, forever

Everything accumulates into a living timeline — a record of who they actually were, in their own era, told in your words. Pebble by pebble, a book takes shape.

It's impossible to cherish every stone, gravel and rock that make up a childhood.
But we can try to save some of the pebbles.

How it works

Four steps to a childhood remembered

1

Sign up your children

Create profiles for one child or all of them. PebbleBook is ready for the whole family from day one.

2

Answer a prompt

Choose a child and we'll offer an age-tailored question. Swipe if it doesn't spark anything — we'll find one that does. Or switch to sibling mode and answer for them all at once.

3

We nudge, not shame

Set the commitment level that suits your life. We'll send a gentle reminder now and then — we're here to help, not to judge.

4

Watch the story grow

Everything you write lives in a timeline — your child's story, written pebble by pebble, in your own words.

Example prompts

Describe what happened the last time your child tried to play with another kid at the park.
Has [NAME] said something funny lately?
