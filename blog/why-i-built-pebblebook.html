Why I Built PebbleBook: The Fear of Forgetting (And the Sibling Gap)

Published August 2026 • 5 min read

A few years ago, I convinced myself I would remember everything. Every mispronounced word, every bedtime routine, every sudden obsession with a specific shirt or storybook. When you’re living in those exhausting, vivid days, it feels impossible to imagine ever forgetting them.

And then time passes. One day you wake up and realize your child speaks clearly, sleeps in a bigger bed, and doesn't ask for that funny game anymore. You try to remember exactly when it changed, or what their voice sounded like when they were three—and the details are hazy.

The Quiet Guilt of Forgetting

The fear of forgetting isn't just about losing dates or statistics. It’s the quiet heartbreak of missing out on the person your child used to be. Parents aren't lazy; we’re just overwhelmed. Traditional baby books ask for pristine memories and exact dates, which leads to blank pages and nagging guilt.

It is impossible to cherish every stone and rock that make up a childhood. But we can save the pebbles—the small, everyday details that capture who they really were.

The "Second Child Gap"

There was another problem I couldn't ignore: the sibling imbalance. With the first child, parents take hundreds of photos and write detailed journal entries. By the time child number two or three arrives, life is so chaotic that memory-keeping drops off a cliff.

Looking back at an empty baby book for a younger sibling brings a specific kind of guilt. I wanted a way to make sure every child has their story told, without requiring double or triple the effort from exhausted parents.

"Every child deserves to read their own story one day—not just the firstborn."

Why PebbleBook Exists

That is why PebbleBook was born. I wanted to design a tool built for real parenting:

  • Zero Guilt: No red notifications, blank page shame, or pressure to write long entries.
  • Sibling Mode: Answer one gentle prompt in 30 seconds, and let the app automatically file responses into each child’s individual book.
  • A Living Timeline: Watch small moments accumulate into a beautiful PDF export or printed keepsake over time.

PebbleBook exists so you never have to look back and wonder who your children were before they grew up. 30 seconds at a time, we keep the pebbles safe.
