Design Principles & Features

built for real life with children.

Most baby books fail because they demand too much time and energy. PebbleBook is engineered around the reality of daily parenting life.

01 / CAPTURE Evening Routine

Write a sentence or pick a prompt

Open the app at bedtime. Record a funny phrase, a cozy detail, or let a targeted age-appropriate question guide your memory in under 90 seconds.

“What did Finn refuse to take off today?”
02 / SIBLING EQUITY Multi-Child

Tag one child or all of them

Eliminate the second-child gap. Easily assign an entry to one specific child or tag all siblings at once when answering joint family prompts.

Lea Finn Nora
03 / GUILT-FREE Zero Streaks

No streak counts or red badges

Life with kids is unpredictable. Skip three weeks without penalty or guilt. Your pebble timeline is designed to look complete regardless of gaps.

04 / PRINT & KEEP Physical Heirloom

Export to a bound hardcover book

When you're ready, turn digital entries into a beautifully designed physical book for your child to read when they grow up.
