Design Principles & Features

a journal built for exhausted parents.

Most baby books fail because they demand too much time and energy. PebbleBook is engineered around the reality of daily parenting life.

01 / Quiet Design

The 90-second evening ritual.

No endless feeds, notifications, or social features. Open PebbleBook at bedtime, capture a moment in under two minutes, and close your phone for the night.

  • Dark-mode friendly interface for bedside writing
  • Low cognitive load with single-line entry support
  • Intelligent age-matched prompts when you're stuck
Finn Evening prompt

“What made everyone laugh out loud today at the dinner table?”

02 / Sibling Equity

Fairness across 1 to 4 children.

Firstborn children usually have shelves of photo albums and journals. Later siblings often receive far less. PebbleBook lets you tag shared moments across multiple children with a single tap.

  • Joint family prompts that attach to multiple child profiles
  • Individual color pills for easy visual filtering
  • Separate hardcover book exports per child

03 / Zero Guilt

No streaks. No badges. No guilt.

Parenting is unpredictable. If you miss days or weeks, PebbleBook never punishes you with red notification dots or broken streak counters. Your timeline seamlessly fills in whenever you return.

  • Fluid layout automatically handles missing dates gracefully
  • No aggressive push notifications or streak counters
  • Pick back up at any time without feeling behind
