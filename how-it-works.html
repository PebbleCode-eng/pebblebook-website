Our Philosophy & App Features

Built for real life with children — right on your phone

PebbleBook is launching on the Apple App Store and Google Play Store in Fall 2026 / Winter 2027. Designed to eliminate parent guilt by turning tiny daily moments into long-term memories.

1

Create profiles on iOS or Android

Download the app and register your children in seconds. Each child gets their own customizable profile, visual timeline feed, and milestone tracker.

2

Answer age-tailored prompts

Receive notifications tailored to your child’s current age and developmental phase. Use Sibling Mode to answer a single question for all your children at once—our app automatically sorts each response into the right child's individual story feed.

"Describe what happened the last time your child tried to play with someone at the park."
"What did [NAME] insist on wearing today?"
3

Gentle notifications — zero guilt

Configure your reminder preferences to suit your family’s routine. Missed a couple of weeks? PebbleBook never displays red warnings or blank page shame. Pick up smoothly whenever you have 30 spare seconds.

4

Visual timelines & PDF exports (Physical books coming soon!)

Explore your interactive, chronological timeline inside the app anytime. Want a physical copy? Choose a start and end date to instantly generate and download a print-ready PDF memory book. Roadmap feature: Direct ordering for premium hardcover coffee-table albums will be integrated straight into the app!
