How it works

Four steps to a childhood remembered

PebbleBook is designed to fit into real life — not demand more of it.

1

Sign up and register your children

Create a profile for one child or all of them at once. Each child gets their own colour, their own timeline, their own story. You can add more children any time — PebbleBook is built for families that grow.

2

Choose a child and answer a prompt

We give you age-tailored questions that spark the kind of memories you'd otherwise forget. If one doesn't land, swipe left — we'll find another. Switch to sibling mode to answer for all your children at once, and we'll save each response individually.

"Describe what happened the last time your child tried to play with another kid at the park."
"Has [NAME] said something funny lately?"
3

We nudge — we never shame

You choose the commitment level that works for your life. We'll send a gentle reminder when it feels right. We're here to help you capture what you can, not to make you feel bad about what you missed.

4

Watch the story grow

Everything you write lives in a timeline — your child's story, in your words, sorted by date. Over time it becomes something extraordinary: a record of who they actually were, in their own era. Pebble by pebble, a book takes shape.
